profiles, and visualization outputs for revenue-driven marketing strategies.

================================================================================
ALGORITHM SUMMARY
================================================================================

Stage 1: Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering
  - Impute missing Income (median)
  - Remove Age > 90, Income > 600k outliers
  - Create Age, Total_Spending, Total_Children features
  - Consolidate Education (5→3 categories)
  - Consolidate Marital_Status (8→2 categories)

Stage 2: Encoding & Scaling
  - One-Hot Encoding (categorical features)
  - StandardScaler normalization (mean=0, sd=1)

Stage 3: Dimensionality Reduction
  - PCA with 9 components (84.2% variance)

Stage 4: Clustering & Validation
  - K-Means with Elbow Method (K=1-10)
  - Silhouette Score analysis (K=2-10)
  - Agglomerative Hierarchical (n_clusters=4, Ward linkage)

Stage 5: Analysis & Visualization
  - Cluster characterization (mean statistics)
  - 3D PCA visualization
  - Cluster size distribution
  - Income vs Spending scatter plot


================================================================================
KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. 4 distinct customer segments identified
2. High positive correlation: Income vs Total_Spending (>0.8)
3. Recency shows segment differentiation (churn risk indicator)
4. PCA 9 components sufficient for 84%+ variance
5. Ward linkage produces more balanced cluster distribution than K-Means
